# Forest Bathing (Shinrin-yoku) - Research & Website Strategy

## What is Forest Bathing?
**Shinrin-yoku** (translated as "taking in the forest atmosphere" or "forest bathing") is a Japanese eco-therapy practice developed in the 1980s. 

You were spot-on that it involves activity, but the crucial distinction is that **it is not a workout or a hike**. 
- **The Core Concept**: Moving deliberately and slowly through a natural environment to shift from a mindset of "doing" to "being".
- **The Mechanism**: Immersing the five senses (sight, smell, touch, sound, taste) in nature to foster a state of mindfulness. 
- **The Science**: The practice has been proven to lower heart rates, reduce blood pressure, decrease the stress hormone cortisol, and stimulate the immune system (partly due to breathing in "phytoncides"—antimicrobial organic compounds emitted by trees).

- **Typography:** Clean, organic sans-serifs (like *Outfit* or *Inter*) or elegant transitional serifs to convey groundedness.

- **Pacing:** Animations for text elements entering the screen should be significantly slower and softer than standard websites, mimicking the "slow down" philosophy of the practice.
