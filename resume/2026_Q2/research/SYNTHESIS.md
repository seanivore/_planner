## The five highest-leverage findings

1. **"Fractional CMO" alone is now saturated.** 110K+ practitioners on LinkedIn (up from 2K in 2022). The differentiated lane is **niche + modifier**: "Embedded Fractional CMO," "Operating Partner," "Advisor-in-Residence." Pair the SEO-valuable term with an industry × stage × AI-fluency qualifier.

2. **"AI-augmented" wins. "Leveraged AI" loses.** 49% of hiring managers auto-dismiss resumes they suspect were AI-written. The single biggest tell is "Leveraged…" (appears in ~40% of AI-generated resumes). Preferred 2026 framing is *AI-augmented workflow* or *AI-assisted* — both signal human-in-the-loop.

3. **AI goes in three places, never the title.** Value-proposition summary line + dedicated "AI & Creative Tools" skills block above experience + Tool/Task/Outcome bullets inside each role. Don't put "AI" in the title line — reads as compensating.

4. **The seniority tell is owning the problem definition, not the deliverable.** Junior resumes say what they made. Senior resumes say what they *decided*: *"The brand had four conflicting visual languages across product surfaces; I unified them in 90 days."*

5. **Don't self-label "generalist."** The category was rehabilitated only with proof-of-depth. Use **T-shaped with named depth** ("Brand systems designer with social + AI workflow depth"). Let evidence show range.

## The 2026 bullet formula

**Tool + Task + Measurable Outcome + Business Reason**

Models:
- *"Reduced research time by 70% by implementing Claude-powered competitive analysis workflows, enabling faster go-to-market decisions."*
- *"Integrated AI-assisted design workflows using Figma AI and custom GPT tools for content generation, reducing initial wireframing time by 50% and enabling 3x more variations for user testing per sprint."*
- *"Led adoption of Microsoft 365 Copilot across a 40-person team, developing internal training guide and reducing onboarding time by 30%."*

For Sean specifically — the strongest base material to retrofit into this formula:
- PETA: $0.002 CPA via Optimizely A/B testing across 3.2B FB impressions
- PETA: scaled digital team 2→13 (with that headcount, the formula becomes "*led* a team of 13" — senior verb on team-scaling)
- August House: $0.31/follow organic on TikTok/IG, $0.003 CPE Meta ads, 80+ weekly agentic blog posts at $3.58 CPM
- 360-design: 37-entry portfolio system with agentic entry-creation SOP cutting per-entry time from hours to minutes
- Everlastings: complete e-commerce stack at ~$15–75/yr operating cost, non-technical client self-manages

## Headline / summary formula

Winning 2026 pattern: **Problem Solver + Industry + Secret Weapon**, first-person verb.

- *"Revenue Growth Architect | B2B SaaS | Converting Data Into 7-Figure Campaigns"*
- *"I Build Scalable Revenue Engines for FinTech Startups | ABM, Marketing Ops & GTM Strategy"*
- *"15 years building high-performing sales teams in SaaS"* (Corby Fine's "expertise over employer" — survives job changes)

Avoid:
- Job-title-pipe-tools formats ("Senior Designer | Figma, Adobe CC, Webflow")
- Objective statements ("Seeking a challenging role…")
- Trait claims ("Results-driven, passionate, detail-oriented")
