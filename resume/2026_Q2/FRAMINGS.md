## Cross-variant constraints (every writer must honor)

- **One page, ~650–700 words.** Match `resume/TEMPLATE_COPY.md` section order.
- **Every Experience role has at least one numeric metric.** No bare adjective bullets.
- **No duplicated bullets across roles.** Each role earns its own material.
- **Three places for AI** (skills block + summary line + 1–2 bullets) — **never in the title line**.
- **Scrub the banned words** (see SYNTHESIS.md — *leveraged, spearheaded, results-driven, passionate about, delve, realm, intricate, showcasing, pivotal,* etc.).
- **Bullet formula:** Tool + Task + Outcome + Business reason. Prefer *"reduced X from Y to Z"* over bare percentages.
- **Senior verbs used sparingly:** *architected, orchestrated, established, led adoption of, unified, productized, partnered with*.
- **No self-labeling "generalist."** Show range through evidence.
- **Header line is identical across all six:** `Sean August Horvath | sean@august.style | (424) 744–7687 | www.august.style`.
