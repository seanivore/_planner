## Phrasings that landed unexpectedly well across multiple variants

Patterns the writers and reviewers converged on independently — these are signals worth keeping in any final version:

- **"Three concurrent embedded engagements/builds"** — appears across V2, V4, V5, V6. Reads as senior-fractional cadence.
- **"Early to the tools that mattered"** (V6) — Optimizely (2014) → Web3 (2020) → MCP (2024) as a single through-line. Powerful frame for a career arc.
- **"From hours to minutes" / "From X to Y"** — appears across V2, V3, V5, V6. Beats bare percentage every time.
- **"Reinvested viral-success revenue to hire 15 additional creators"** — V3, V5. Senior-coded language; converts a creative metric into a P&L narrative.
- **"Adobe pipelines / inside Photoshop and InDesign"** when naming AI — V1. Makes Firefly/Midjourney read as craft tools, not buzzwords.
- **"Productized" as a verb** — V1, V3, V4, V5. The 2026 equivalent of "scaled" or "operationalized" but with stronger commercial signal.
