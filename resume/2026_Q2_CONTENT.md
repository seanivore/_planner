Before starting I would like some brainstorming on what you can research about finding jobs right now, then delegate that research to subagents. When they come back with information, how about then you analyze it and come up with different framing of the resume, in addition to the proposed above, and then assign them to subagents to write. I think then we should consider some kind of subagent review where a clean context (or primed context) subagent would review and then take it upon themselves to immediately improve the resume versions that the first round of subagents created. Afterwards, I'd like for you to read them all and present them to me with some kind of matrix that explains the pros and cons of each version, from different perspectives, as well as which types of jobs each version would be best suited for, etc. Also a summary, using the term loosely, for each that pulls out really strong wording, soundbites, framing, etc. anything notable that might be worth pulling into a final few versions that I can propose after reviewing everything and we decide on a direction. 

Please think hard and be creative with this. I'm confident in my skills, but I know that the job market is a bit difficult right now, so I want to make sure that I'm presenting myself in the best possible way and it seems like an exciting and innovative idea to AI crowd source tactics and even just soundbites. 

LMK what you think. I provided a TON of historic resume backlog data, and even entries to my portfolios. Those are a lot of information so we should do batches of subagents who do and who don't get all that information — if you agree that that makes strategic sense. 

Open to other ideas — thanks for the help, I'm excited to see what we can come up with.
